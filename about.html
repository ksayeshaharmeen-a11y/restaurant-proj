About Our Story

Some kitchens cook food. Ours cooks memories.

Asha's Kitchen was born in a small home kitchen in Ambur - from a mother's love, a family recipe book, and a dream to serve the world on one table. Today, we bring Seoul's fire, Shanghai's soul, and Ambur's heart together in every single plate.

No frozen food. No shortcuts. Only fresh spices, slow cooking, and recipes passed down with love - just like home, but with a taste of the world.

  • 🔥 Korean Soul - Real Gochujang, Kimchi and BBQ, made with patience
  • 🥟 Chinese Craft - Hand-folded Dumplings and Wok-tossed Noodles, fresh every day
  • 🍛 Indian Heart - Ambur Special Biryani, Tandoor and Slow-Cooked Curries
